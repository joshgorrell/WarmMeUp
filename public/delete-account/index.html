WARM ME UP

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Warm Me Up account at any time. Account deletion removes your profile and associated app data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Delete from the app

Open Warm Me Up, then go to Profile → Settings → Delete My Account and confirm deletion.

No longer have the app?

You can still request permanent account deletion without reinstalling Warm Me Up. Email our support team from the email address associated with your Warm Me Up account, or identify that account email in your request. We may contact the account email to verify ownership before completing deletion.

Request Account Deletion

What is deleted

  • Your Warm Me Up profile and account settings.
  • Your messages, interactions, dares, wishes, notes, and related activity.
  • Vault media and other content associated with your account, subject to any limited retention required by law or for security/fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Your active partner connection.

Deleting your Warm Me Up account does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Subscription management remains with the applicable app store.
