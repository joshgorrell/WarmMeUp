# Discreet Notifications info sheet copy

**Discreet Notifications**

When turned **on**, Warm Me Up keeps your notifications private by showing only **“New Activity.”**

When turned **off**, notifications can tell you what type of activity you received, such as **“New Message,” “New Picture,” or “New Video”** — but they’ll never show the actual message, photo, video, or other private content.

**Example**

Discreet ON → *New Activity*  
Discreet OFF → *New Picture*

**Your private content stays inside Warm Me Up.**
