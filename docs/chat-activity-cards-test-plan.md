# Chat activity cards test plan

1. Send a normal Chat message and confirm existing rendering, reactions, replies, media, and burn behavior are unchanged.
2. Share a new Wish and confirm a Wish card appears in Chat for both partners without a duplicate normal text bubble.
3. Create a Wish as draft and confirm no Chat card appears until the Wish is shared.
4. Send a Dare and confirm a Dare card appears in chronological order and opens the originating Dare.
5. Roll Dice for the partner and confirm a Dice card appears and opens the originating roll.
6. Keep Chat open on the receiving device while sending a Wish/Dare/Dice from the partner and confirm the card appears through the existing Chat realtime subscription.
7. Confirm activity cards are not editable/replyable as ordinary messages.
8. Confirm older Chat pagination continues to work.
9. Confirm Burn/delete operations do not crash when activity-card rows are present.
